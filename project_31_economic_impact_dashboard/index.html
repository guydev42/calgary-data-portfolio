Project 31

Economic impact dashboard

Environmental changes have direct economic consequences for Alberta's resource-dependent communities. This project merges ODAA's environmental and socioeconomic datasets to quantify how land degradation, wildfire events, and vegetation loss correlate with economic indicators like property values, employment, and business activity across six regions.

Approach

Methodology

Merged ODAA environmental datasets (NDVI, land surface temperature, fire frequency, precipitation) with socioeconomic indicators (property assessments, employment rates, business registrations) from Alberta government sources. Built regional panel regression models to quantify how environmental degradation impacts economic output. Created an interactive what-if simulator that lets stakeholders explore counterfactual scenarios. The executive summary page auto-generates a narrative report with key findings translated into plain-language business recommendations.
